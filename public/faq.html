💳 Ordering & Payment

  • We accept a wide range of payment options for your convenience:
    • UPI — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI
    • Credit & Debit Cards — Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express
    • Net Banking — All major Indian banks supported
    • Cash on Delivery (COD) — Available for orders below ₹10,000
    • EMI — No-cost EMI on select cards for orders above ₹3,000
    • Wallets — Paytm, Mobikwik, FreeCharge
    All online transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Yes! Cash on Delivery is available for most pin codes across India for orders below ₹10,000. To check COD availability at your pin code, simply add items to your cart and proceed to checkout — the system will automatically indicate if COD is available for your address. A nominal COD handling fee of ₹49 may apply on some orders.
  • To apply a coupon code:
    • Add your desired products to the cart.
    • Go to the Shopping Cart page.
    • Enter your coupon code in the "Apply Coupon" field.
    • Click Apply — the discount will reflect instantly.
    Please note that only one coupon can be applied per order. Some coupons have minimum order value requirements or are valid only on specific product categories.
  • Orders can be modified or cancelled within 24 hours of placement, provided they have not yet been shipped. To request a cancellation:
    • Log into your account and go to My Orders.
    • Select the order and click "Cancel Order".
    • Choose your reason and confirm.
    If your order has already been dispatched, cancellation is not possible. However, you may initiate a return once you receive the product. For urgent requests, contact our support team immediately.
  • Don't worry — this is a common banking-side delay. If your payment failed but money was debited from your account:
    • Wait 4–5 business days for an automatic reversal by your bank.
    • If not reversed, email us at support@rjfashion.com with your transaction ID and bank statement screenshot.
    • We will raise a dispute with the payment gateway and ensure a full refund within 7–10 business days.
  • Yes, we provide GST-compliant tax invoices for all orders. Your invoice is automatically generated after the order is confirmed and can be downloaded from My Orders. If you need a business GST invoice with your company's GSTIN, please add it during checkout in the billing details section or contact us within 24 hours of ordering.
  • Absolutely. We use industry-standard PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways and never store your full card number on our servers. Saved card details are securely tokenized by our payment partners (Razorpay/PayU). You can manage or delete saved payment methods anytime from your Account Settings.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery

  • Delivery timelines vary by location:
    • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2–4 business days
    • Tier-2 Cities: 4–6 business days
    • Remote / Tier-3 Areas: 6–10 business days
    Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for select pin codes for an additional charge. Delivery times may be longer during festive seasons (Diwali, Navratri, Eid) due to high demand.
  • Yes! We offer Free Express Shipping on all orders above ₹999 across India. For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹79 applies. Premium sarees and heavy jewelry pieces may have special packaging charges of ₹49, which will be shown clearly at checkout.
  • Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:
    • An email and SMS with a tracking link and AWB (Air Waybill) number.
    • You can also track your order live from My Orders in your account.
    We use trusted courier partners including BlueDart, Delhivery, Ekart, and India Post for reliable delivery pan India.
  • Our courier partners typically make 3 delivery attempts on consecutive days. If all attempts fail, your package will be held at the nearest courier facility for 7 days for self-pickup. After 7 days, the package is returned to us and a re-delivery fee will apply. To avoid this, please ensure someone is available at the delivery address, or contact our support team to arrange a convenient delivery slot.
  • Currently, RJ Fashion Collection ships across all states and union territories within India. International shipping to select countries (USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Australia, Singapore) is available for orders above ₹5,000. International shipping charges and timelines (typically 10–20 business days) vary by destination. Please contact us for international shipping quotes and customs information.
  • We apologize for this experience. If your order arrives damaged or defective:
    • Do NOT accept the package if it appears severely tampered. Note the refusal on the courier delivery sheet.
    • If you have already accepted it, take clear photos/video of the damage.
    • Email support@rjfashion.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos.
    We will arrange a free pickup and full replacement or refund within 5–7 business days.
  • Address changes are possible only if the order has not yet been dispatched. To request a change, go to My Orders and use the "Edit Address" option, or contact our support team immediately with your order ID and the corrected address. Once dispatched, we are unable to reroute shipments.

✨ Products & Quality

  • Yes, absolutely. We source directly from certified weavers and artisan clusters across India — including Varanasi (Banarasi), Kanchipuram, Chanderi, and Sambalpuri. Each premium saree comes with a Silk Mark India certification tag verifying its authenticity. Our quality control team physically inspects every piece before dispatch to ensure it meets our luxury standards.
  • We use professional photography under calibrated studio lighting to ensure color accuracy. However, colors may appear slightly different depending on your screen's calibration, brightness, and display settings. We always include multiple angles and closeup detail shots to give you the best possible view. If you're uncertain about a color, contact our team — we can send additional photos or fabric swatches on request for orders above ₹5,000.
  • We have a comprehensive Size Guide with detailed measurement charts for all our product categories — including saree blouses, kurtis, lehengas, and even jewelry sizes. We strongly recommend measuring your bust, waist, and hip before ordering. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up. Custom tailoring is also available on select products — look for the "Customize Size" option on the product page.
  • Silk sarees require gentle care to maintain their luster and longevity:
    • Dry clean only for pure Kanjivaram and Banarasi silks.
    • For semi-silk or art silk sarees, hand-wash in cold water with mild silk-safe detergent.
    • Never machine wash or tumble dry silk sarees.
    • Do not wring or squeeze — gently roll in a soft dry towel to remove moisture.
    • Dry in shade (never direct sunlight) to prevent color fading.
    • Store wrapped in muslin cloth in a cool, dry place. Avoid plastic covers.
    Care instructions are also printed on the tag attached to your garment.
  • We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return:
    • The item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags intact.
    • Initiate the return from My Orders within 7 days.
    • Our team will schedule a free pickup within 48 hours.
    • Refund will be processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item.
    Non-returnable items: Customized products, intimate wear, accessories in opened packaging, and items purchased during clearance sales (marked "Final Sale").
  • Yes! We offer customization services for select products:
    • Blouse stitching — Custom measurements, neck design, sleeve style (add-on: ₹299–₹799)
    • Fall & Pico — Professional saree finishing (₹99)
    • Embroidery additions — Additional zari, thread, or mirror work on request
    To request customization, contact us after placing your order or look for the "Add Customization" option on the product page. Customized orders have a delivery timeline of 7–14 days and are not eligible for returns unless defective.
  • Yes! We offer premium gift packaging for an additional ₹99 per item. This includes a beautiful branded box, tissue wrapping, a personalized greeting card, and a satin ribbon. You can select gift packaging during checkout and add a personal message. It's perfect for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, anniversaries, or any celebration. We also offer corporate bulk gifting — contact us for bulk orders.

👤 Account & Profile

  • Creating an account is free and easy:
    • Click Login / Account in the top navigation.
    • Select "Create New Account".
    • Enter your name, email address, and a secure password.
    • Verify your email via the link we send you.
    With an account, you can track orders, manage wishlists, save addresses, view purchase history, and get exclusive member-only offers.
  • To reset your password:
    • Go to Login and click "Forgot Password?"
    • Enter your registered email address.
    • Check your inbox for a password reset link (valid for 1 hour).
    • Click the link and enter your new password.
    If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam/junk folder. Still having trouble? Contact support.
  • You can save multiple delivery addresses to your account for convenient checkout. To manage addresses:
    • Log in and go to My Account.
    • Select "Manage Addresses".
    • Add, edit, or delete saved addresses.
    • Set a "Default Address" for faster checkout.
    You can save home, office, and other addresses separately.
  • Your Wishlist lets you save products you love for later. Simply click the ♡ heart icon on any product to add it. You must be logged in to save your wishlist permanently. Currently, wishlists are private to your account — sharing directly from the website is coming soon. In the meantime, you can copy and share the product page URL with friends or family.
  • To update your contact information:
    • Log in and go to My Account → Edit Profile.
    • Update your email address or phone number.
    • Email changes require verification via a link sent to the new address.
    • Phone number changes may require OTP verification.
    For security, we limit profile changes to 3 updates per 30 days. If you face issues, contact support.
  • You can request account deletion by emailing privacy@rjfashion.com from your registered email with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process the request within 7 business days and send a confirmation. Please note:
    • All your order history, wishlist, and saved data will be permanently deleted.
    • Any pending orders must be resolved before deletion.
    • Active loyalty points will be forfeited upon deletion.
    This action cannot be undone.
  • To manage your communication preferences:
    • Click the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any promotional email.
    • Or go to My Account → Notification Settings → toggle off "Promotional Emails" and "SMS Alerts".
    You will continue to receive essential transactional messages (order confirmations, shipping updates) even after unsubscribing from promotions. Unsubscription takes effect within 48 hours.
