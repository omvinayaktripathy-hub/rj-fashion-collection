💡 Why does Firebase Console (settings/cloudmessaging) look empty?

In the new Google Firebase Console, the Cloud Messaging page does not show data until you generate your Web Push Certificate. Here is how to complete it in 30 seconds:

  1. Open Firebase Console: Cloud Messaging
  2. Scroll down to "Web configuration" section.
  3. Under "Web Push certificates", click the white button: "Generate key pair".
  4. That creates your live public key pair (VAPID key) so Chrome, Safari, and Edge can deliver push notifications directly!
